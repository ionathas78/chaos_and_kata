# Stone Temple Rising

A breath-counted yoga routine built around a grounded opening, mirrored Warrior phrases, strength holds, and a floor sequence that rises into a closing Sun Salutation.

## Breath Cadence

- Hold each pose for **5 breaths** unless otherwise noted.
- **Plank:** 7 breaths.
- **Bridge:** 7 breaths.
- Move continuously between poses where practical.

## Opening

1. Mountain — 5 breaths
2. Forward Fold — 5 breaths
3. Chair — 5 breaths
4. Garland — 5 breaths

## First Side

1. Warrior I — 5 breaths
2. Warrior II — 5 breaths
3. Triangle — 5 breaths
4. Warrior II — 5 breaths
5. Warrior III — 5 breaths
6. Downward Dog — 5 breaths
7. Plank — **7 breaths**
8. Upward Dog — 5 breaths
9. Lunge — 5 breaths

## Mirror

Repeat the entire Warrior/vinyasa phrase on the opposite side:

1. Warrior I — 5 breaths
2. Warrior II — 5 breaths
3. Triangle — 5 breaths
4. Warrior II — 5 breaths
5. Warrior III — 5 breaths
6. Downward Dog — 5 breaths
7. Plank — **7 breaths**
8. Upward Dog — 5 breaths
9. Lunge — 5 breaths

## Floor Sequence

1. Hero — 5 breaths
2. Reclined Hero — 5 breaths
3. Bridge — **7 breaths**
4. Seated Goddess — 5 breaths
5. Child's Pose — 5 breaths

## Closing

1. Forward Fold — 5 breaths
2. Sun Salutation — 5 breaths
